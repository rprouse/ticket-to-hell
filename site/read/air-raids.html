Air Raids

Comparison

England

In 1940, air raids were infrequent until August and September, when the ‘blitz’ commenced in daylight raids over all of England, but mainly over the southeast coast, London and the Midlands. About October, 1940, the tactics changed to night bombing, which lasted throughout the winter until the spring of 1941, when raids eased off to ‘nuisance’ or ‘hit and run’ raids, mainly on the southeast coast. These raids lasted through to 1942 and were still going strong when we sailed for France in August 1942.

Germany

From August, 1942 to December, 1943, air raids where we were imprisoned were infrequent. (Excepting for the time spent in a French hospital in ‘Rouen-sur-Seine’, where raids occurred nightly.) Heavy raids in our area of Thüringen commenced January 1, 1944.
