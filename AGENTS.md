# AGENTS.md: Grad's Book digitization

This folder digitizes *Ticket to Hell via Dieppe: From a Prisoner's Wartime Log, 1942–1945* by A. Robert Prouse (Van Nostrand Reinhold / Webb & Bower, 1982). It is a family project run by the author's grandson, Rob. The book is a Canadian Provost Corps soldier's memoir of his capture at Dieppe on August 19, 1942, his time as a POW in Stalag IXC (Molsdorf, Niederorschel, Mühlhausen and Bad Sulza), two escapes, and liberation in April 1945.

Treat the text with care. This is a grandfather's own account, so the goal is a faithful, clean edition, not a rewrite.

## Folder layout

Files are named by section. `1 - A Raid on Dieppe.txt` through `9 - Forced March to Liberation.txt` are the chapters. The other sections are `Prologue`, `Epilogue`, `Barbed-Wire Ballads`, `End Notes` and `Front Notes and Contents`.

## Book order

Front matter, then:

1. Prologue
2. Chapters 1 to 9
3. Epilogue
4. Appendices: Barbed-Wire Ballads, German Orders, Air Raids, A Note on Photos, Mementos and Artifacts, and Organized Compassion

All the appendices after the Ballads live in `End Notes.txt`, under section headings that match the Contents page.

## Text file conventions

- UTF-8 **with BOM**, **CRLF** line endings. Keep both.
- Each paragraph is one line, with a blank line between paragraphs. Chapter files open with `Chapter N`, a blank line, then the title.
- Verse in `Barbed-Wire Ballads.txt` keeps one line per verse line. The prose notes between poems are reflowed into single lines.
- Use curly quotes: single `‘ ’` for dialogue, as in the book, and `’` for apostrophes. Use em dashes `—` for breaks and hyphens in compounds (`box-cars`, `twenty-one`).
- Use British/Canadian spelling: colour, honour, kilometres, manoeuvres.
- German place names take umlauts: Mühlhausen, Düren, Köln, Düsseldorf, München-Gladbach, Sömmerda, Thüringen. The exception is `Grossneuhausen`, which keeps "ss".

## Editorial rules

There are two kinds of change, and they must stay distinguishable in `Review Notes.md`:

- **Transcription fixes:** the file doesn't match the printed page. Always fix these.
- **Editorial corrections:** the 1982 printing itself is wrong (misspelled places, wrong German, typos). Rob approved correcting place names and German. Log each one so it can be reverted.

**Keep as the author wrote them.** These are deliberate, not errors:

- `laager` for camp, used consistently.
- `Jerrys`.
- `Nixverstein`, POW slang for "nicht verstehen".
- `Allus kaput`, the foreman's dialect.
- `strafe`, meaning punishment.
- `Quonset hut`.
- `confidant man`, the camp's go-between with the Germans.
- The broken English in the German notices in End Notes, including `matchen`, `boundarys`, `expecially`, `paiement` and `thread-hedge`.

**Flag, don't silently fix.** Factual or chronological doubts go in `Review Notes.md` under "Flagged but not changed", with how confident you are. The open items so far:

- Stalag VIG versus VI H at Arnoldsweiler.
- Zweilager versus Zweiglager.
- Hottenhausen.
- SS Vullendam versus Volendam.
- The raid counts in chapter 8 versus the Air Raids table.
- "March 20" appearing out of order in chapter 8.
- The Arnhem timing.

Ask Rob before resolving any of these.

Nicknames are hyphenated and consistent: `Plank-face`, `Crazy-legs`, `Gee-guts`, `Lard-ass`, `Rat Face`, `Boss Eye`.

## Verifying against the scans

- When something looks odd, check the scan before changing it. Render with `pdftoppm -r 250 -gray`, OCR with `tesseract`, and view the page image for anything close.
- Known OCR failure patterns from earlier passes:
  - `1` for `l`: `cou1dn’t`, `tl1e`.
  - `fl`/`f` read as `H` or `fi`: `Hat car` for flat car, `Hue` for flue, `Hash` for flash.
  - `fighter` read as `lighter`.
  - `comer` for corner.
  - `ü` read as `ii` (`Miihlhausen`).
  - Numbers read as letters (`rr` for 11, `ztst` for 21st, `t79` for 179).
  - Stray margin and gutter characters such as `Q I E p g l \` ~ ¤ { }`.
- **Paragraph breaks:** the original conversion started a paragraph at every printed line that ended in a period. The real paragraph starts are the indented lines in the scan. Measure the line's left edge in tesseract TSV output; real indents are about 35–45 px at 250 dpi.
- Photo captions, maps, posters and document images are **deliberately not in the text files**. They live in `Images/`, with captions and placement in `Image Notes.md`, so don't mix captions into the narrative.

## Images

- Images are named `NN-MM_slug.jpg`. `NN` is the section: 00 cover, 01–09 chapters, 10 Epilogue, 11 appendices.
- Captions are the printed ones, with leading direction words ("Left:", "Above:") removed. Each entry gives both the printed book position and, where the image illustrates a different passage, a suggested position. Positions are given by the opening words of the paragraph the image follows in `TXT - Cleaned/`. The suggested position is the default for an illustrated EPUB; ask Rob if unsure.
- Images without a printed caption still need one: 00-cover, 04-01, 07-03 to 07-05, 07-08, 11-01 and 11-02. Don't invent factual details for these.
- 07-08 is a typeset text box and is better set as a text sidebar than as an image.
- Spreads (03-02, 03-11, 04-03, 05-02, 08-03) are two scan pages joined together.
- The audiobook EPUB has no images. An illustrated edition is a separate build.
- **How to re-crop:** photo pages in `Scans/` are embedded 200 dpi JPEGs, while text and document pages are 1-bit at 400 dpi. Pull photo pages losslessly with `pdfimages -j`. Render 1-bit document pages with `pdftoppm -r 300 -gray`. Each entry in `Image Notes.md` names its source scan page.

## Releases (GitHub)

The repository is `rprouse/ticket-to-hell` on GitHub, and the default branch is `main`. `.github/workflows/release.yml` publishes a new GitHub release **only for pushes to `main`** that change one of these:

- `Ticket to Hell via Dieppe - Audiobook.epub`, `- Illustrated.epub` or `- Text Only.epub`
- `Ticket to Hell via Dieppe - Illustrated.pdf`
- anything in `Audiobook/`

It can also be run by hand from the Actions tab ("Run workflow"), but the job refuses to run on any branch other than `main`.

- **Assets:** the three EPUBs and the PDF, renamed without spaces (for example `Ticket-to-Hell-via-Dieppe-Illustrated.epub`). The audiobook is attached as one ZIP, `Ticket-to-Hell-via-Dieppe-Audiobook-MP3.zip`, holding every MP3 plus `cover.png`, a copy of `Audiobook Cover.png`. The ZIP is stored rather than compressed, because MP3s don't shrink.
- **Tag and title:** the tag is `vYYYY.MM.DD-<run number>`, and the title gives the date and build number.
- **Release notes:** they start with `Description.txt`, then list the downloads, then the edition files changed in the push (found with the GitHub compare API, so no deep clone is needed).
- **Not rebuilt in CI:** the workflow attaches the files as committed; it doesn't regenerate them. Rebuild locally with the `EPUB Build` scripts, commit the outputs, then push to `main`.
- **Size limits:** GitHub caps each file in the repo at 100 MB, and the largest MP3 is about 57 MB. Each release asset can be up to 2 GB.
- `*.zip` is in `.gitignore`, so the audiobook ZIP is only ever built in CI and never committed.

## Illustrated EPUB edition

`Ticket to Hell via Dieppe - Illustrated.epub` is the reading edition. Rebuild it after any change to the text, the images or their captions:

```
python "EPUB Build/build_epub.py"
```

To build the text-only edition (`Ticket to Hell via Dieppe - Text Only.epub`), run the same command with `--no-images`. Rebuild both editions whenever the text changes.

The text-only edition has the same text, front matter and cover, but none of the pictures, captions or transcriptions. It also drops the appendix "A Note on Photos, Mementos and Artifacts", which is about the pictures. It keeps the 07-08 "POW Amusements & Pastimes" sidebar, since that is text in the printed book.

The build needs Python 3.8+ and Pillow (`pip install pillow`), and nothing else (no pandoc). It writes the EPUB to the book folder and stops with an error if an image can't be placed.

**Inputs:**

| Input | What it supplies |
|:--|:--|
| `TXT - Cleaned/*.txt` | The text |
| `Images/images.json` and the `.jpg` files | Titles, captions, image types and transcriptions |
| `EPUB Build/placements.json` | Where each image goes |
| `EPUB Build/style.css` | Styling |
| `Book Cover.png` | Cover artwork; the file name is set by `COVER_FILE` in `build_epub.py` |

**Moving an image:** edit its entry in `placements.json`, then rebuild.
- The `section` field names the text file it belongs to, minus `.txt`. The appendices are named "Note on Photos" and "Organized Compassion".
- Then give either `"at": "start"` / `"at": "end"`, or `"after"` with a phrase from the paragraph the image should follow. The phrase only has to appear somewhere in that paragraph.
- `placements.json` uses the suggested positions from `Image Notes.md`, falling back to the printed book's position. The one deliberate exception is 06-06, which stays at its book position.
- `placements.json` controls the build. `Image Notes.md` is the human reference; keep both in step if you change a position.

**Changing a caption:** edit `Images/images.json`, and update `Image Notes.md` to match.
- Images without a printed caption display their title instead.
- Captions written "(Same caption as …)" are left blank under the second image.
- Transcriptions appear as a boxed note under the caption.
- 07-08 is set as a text sidebar, not as an image.

**What the build does:**
- **Images:** downscaled to 1600 px on the longest side, or 2000 px for documents and maps, then re-saved as JPEG at quality 85. The full-size originals stay in `Images/`.
- **Cover:** `Book Cover.png` is used at its own size, with no upscaling, saved as JPEG at quality 90. Art that isn't 2:3 is padded with white to a 2:3 portrait. A 2:3 cover fills the 6×9 PDF cover page exactly. To change the cover, replace the file, or change `COVER_FILE` in `build_epub.py`, then rebuild all three outputs.
- **Contents:** includes a full front matter (title, full copyright notice, dedication), a visible table of contents, and all appendices. The appendices include the daily March 1945 raid table and the Note on Photos, both of which the audiobook leaves out.

**Validation:** check every build with EPUBCheck (`pip install epubcheck`, then run the bundled jar with Java). The current build passes with no errors or warnings.

## Illustrated PDF

```
python "EPUB Build/build_pdf.py"
```

The script **first rebuilds the illustrated EPUB**, then lays that EPUB out for print, so the PDF always matches it. To change the PDF, change the text, images, captions or `placements.json` as for the EPUB, then rerun this script.

**Setup:** `pip install pillow playwright pypdf reportlab`, then `python -m playwright install chromium`.

**How it works:**
- Headless Chromium renders the EPUB's pages on 6×9 in trade-paperback pages. The print-only CSS is in `PRINT_CSS` inside the script. `style.css` is shared with the EPUB, so changes there affect both.
- It makes two passes. The first finds the page each section starts on, using Chromium's PDF outline (this needs `tagged=True`). The second fills those numbers into the contents page. The script stops if the layout shifts between passes.
- Page numbers are stamped on afterwards with reportlab, starting at 1 on the Prologue page; the front matter is unnumbered. pypdf then adds clean bookmarks.

**Layout rules:**
- Figures never split across pages, so some pages end short before a large image. That is normal for a print layout.
- An image directly after a chapter heading is capped at 4.4 in tall, so it fits on the opening page with the heading.
- Long transcriptions, such as 07-01, can run onto the next page.

## Audiobook edition

The EPUB is built from `TXT - Cleaned/` with pandoc: HTML, then `pandoc --split-level=1`. Keep `--toc` off so the table of contents isn't read aloud. Rules for what goes in it:

- **Include:** title page; a short copyright line ("Copyright © A. Robert Prouse 1982. All rights reserved." plus a first-published line); dedication; Prologue; chapters 1 to 9; Epilogue; Barbed-Wire Ballads with the verse line breaks kept; German Orders; Air Raids as prose with the monthly totals written as sentences; Organized Compassion.
- **Exclude:** the Contents page as spoken text; the full legal boilerplate, catalogue data and ISBN; the March 1945 daily raid table; "A Note on Photos, Mementos and Artifacts"; all captions.
- **Avoid all-caps headings and labels:** text-to-speech may spell capitals out letter by letter. Use title or sentence case.
- Chapter headings take the form `Chapter N: Title`.
- Rebuild the EPUB whenever the cleaned text changes.

## Working with Rob

Rob prefers directness and wants to be told explicitly when you're uncertain rather than confident. For anything that changes his grandfather's own words, explain it and let him decide.
