# Ticket to Hell via Dieppe: images and captions

These images were cropped from the original scans in `Scans/`. Each one is listed below with its caption and where it should go in the EPUB. The same information is in `images.json` for building the EPUB by script.

## How to read this

- **Caption** is the printed caption from the 1982 book. Where it began with a direction word (‘Left:’, ‘Above:’, ‘Below:’, ‘Right:’), the word has been removed, since the EPUB layout won’t match the printed page. The original is noted.
- **Book position** is where the printed book put the image. Images there are often a page or two away from the passage they illustrate, or gathered into a section; the 8-page colour section at the end of Chapter 3 is the main example.
- **Suggested position** appears where the image clearly illustrates a different passage. For an EPUB I’d use the suggested position where there is one, and the book position otherwise.
- Placements name the paragraph the image should follow, by its opening words in `TXT - Cleaned/`.
- Images with no caption, marked **No printed caption**, need one written.
- Some images were two-page spreads; the halves are joined, and a thin strip lost in the binding may be missing at the join.
- Resolution is that of the scans: 200 dpi for photo pages and 300 dpi for the black-and-white document pages. That is plenty for an e-reader but not for a high-quality print edition.
- Chapter title pages (plain type) and the scans of the German Orders and Air Raids appendices were not extracted; that text is already in the TXT files.

## Chapter 2: Limping Towards Germany

### 02-01: Wartime log page: the order to tie prisoners’ hands
- **File:** `Images/02-01_wartime-log-page-the-order-to-tie-prisoners-hand.jpg` (document; from Ch2 scan p.2)
- **Caption:** The directive to tie POWs’ hands was contrary to the Geneva Conventions. Canadians captured at Dieppe and sent directly to camps were shackled every day for months.
- **Book position:** Start of Chapter 2
- **Suggested position:** Chapter 3 — after the paragraph beginning ‘The camp at Molsdorf consisted of…’ (where the shackling is described)
- **Notes:** Handwritten page from the author’s wartime log. The officer’s name reads ‘Eisinger’ but is hard to make out.
- **Transcription:** A photostat copy of a Canadian 2nd Div. order, signed by Capt. Eisinger and found on a Canadian officer when captured at Dieppe, Aug. 19, 1942, was published and a copy posted on all Prisoner of War Camps’ order boards throughout Germany. It stated in short — ‘A prisoner of war cage will be established at — All German P.O.W. will be assembled there, for searching and interrogation by the Canadian Provost Corps. Their hands should be tied behind their backs to prevent their destroying of any documents, maps, etc. in their possession.’ etc. etc. (The above order was given as the reason for the German reprisal of chaining all Canadians.) Note — this ‘Raid’ was stated by the German Military H.Q. to be ‘an attempted second front’. Contrary to this was the leaflets that were printed for the French civil population of Dieppe. In short it read:— ‘Keep under cover and do not try to assist our troops in any way — this is not an invasion but a “coup de main”.’
